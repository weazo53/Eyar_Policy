Delete your account and data (English)

App: “Eyar: Gold, Silver & Currency Prices” · com.eyar.goldsilvercurrency

Option 1: in the app (instant)

  1. Open Eyar and sign in.
  2. Open the menu → Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm. If asked to sign in again, sign out, sign back in and repeat (a Google security step).

Option 2: by email or WhatsApp

Email weazo53@gmail.com from the email registered to your account with the subject “Delete Eyar account”, or message us on WhatsApp @weazo33. We complete requests within 7 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email and name) in Firebase Authentication.
  • Your server backup: gold/silver log, price alerts, zakat inputs, settings.
  • Your city, phone number and marketing consent.

What is not deleted automatically

  • Data stored on your device remains until you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Short-lived technical logs at our service providers (such as IP addresses) expire under their policies. We keep no copy of your data after deletion.
